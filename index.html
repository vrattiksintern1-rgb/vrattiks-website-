02 · Identity

Logo & Usage

The Vrattiks mark comes in three color treatments and two lockups. Use the version that gives the strongest contrast against its background, and never modify the mark's proportions or color relationships.

2.1 Icon mark

Gradient — light backgrounds
Primary Color — light backgrounds
White — dark backgrounds

2.2 Full lockup (icon + wordmark)

Gradient — light backgrounds
Primary Color — light backgrounds
White — dark backgrounds

2.3 Clearspace

Minimum clearspace = 1× the height of the icon on all sides. No text, edges, or other elements should enter this zone.

2.4 Don't

✕
Stretch or distort
Always scale proportionally, both axes locked.
✕
Recolor off-brand
Only use approved gradient, lavender, or white.
✕
Place on low contrast
Choose the light/dark variant that stays legible.
✕
Rotate or tilt
The mark always sits upright, never angled.

05 · Voice & Tone

Voice & Tone

Vrattiks speaks to business owners running SMEs across India — not to developers. Every sentence should sound like it's explaining a result, not a technology.

01

Clear over technical

We write for the business owner, not the engineering team. If a sentence needs a technical dictionary, rewrite it.

Avoid
"Our LLM-powered pipeline leverages RAG architecture for contextual inference."
Instead
"Vrattiks reads your documents and answers questions about them instantly."
02

Confident, not hyped

We talk about outcomes — hours saved, steps removed, errors avoided — not about how impressive the AI is.

Avoid
"Revolutionary AI that will transform your business forever."
Instead
"Cuts your invoice processing time from 3 hours to 12 minutes."
03

Approachable, not intimidating

Many clients are new to AI. Tone should feel like a knowledgeable partner, never like it's talking down to them.

Avoid
"You'll need to integrate our API endpoints with your existing stack."
Instead
"We connect it to the tools you already use — no changes on your end."

06 · Components

6.1 Buttons

Primary
The single most important action on a screen — one per view. Brand gradient fill, lifts with the glow shadow on hover.
Secondary
Graphite fill for a strong action that isn't the primary one — e.g. alongside a Primary button in a dialog.
Outline
Lower-emphasis action on light backgrounds — cancel, back, or a secondary link inside a card.
Ghost
The quietest action — dismissive or optional steps, dense toolbars, inline table actions.

07 · Summary

Applying the System

A quick reference for anyone building with the Vrattiks identity — designers, developers, and partners alike.

✓

Do

  • • Use the gradient mark for hero moments; solid lavender or white for smaller, repeated placements.
  • • Keep at least 1× icon-height of clearspace around the logo at all times.
  • • Pair Urbanist headings with IBM Plex Sans body copy — never mix in a third typeface.
  • • Write copy that leads with the outcome, in plain language a business owner would use.
  • • Use semantic colors only for their assigned meaning (success, warning, error, info).
  • • Stick to one Primary button per screen; use Secondary, Outline, or Ghost for everything else.
  • • Build layouts on the 4px spacing scale and the 12-column grid so screens reflow consistently.
✕

Don't

  • • Stretch, skew, rotate, or recolor the logo outside the approved variants.
  • • Place the logo on backgrounds that break contrast or visual legibility.
  • • Introduce generic red/green/yellow — always use the brand-tinted semantic set.
  • • Use technical or jargon-heavy language aimed at developers in customer-facing copy.
  • • Lean on AI hype language ("revolutionary," "game-changing") in place of real outcomes.
  • • Invent new button styles, card shadows, or spacing values outside this system.
